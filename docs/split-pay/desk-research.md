# Split Pay — desk research (sources checked Nov 2026)

Everything on the case study's Research section, with sources. Know these before an interview.

## Moviegoing is a group activity
- **75%** of metro adults aged 18–44 watch movies with family and friends — GroupM India "Profiling Cinemagoers" (1,075 adults, 5 metros).
  https://bestmediainfo.com/insights/groupm-india-unveils-profiling-cinemagoers-report-on-cinema-viewing-habits-of-consumers-8736512
- **60%** of 14,688 Indian cinemagoers enjoy the "going out" experience with family or friends — EY, *The story of film exhibition in India 2025* (surveyed June–July 2025).
  https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-in/pdf/ey-the-story-of-film-exhibition-in-india-2025.pdf
- Older signal: friends are the favourite movie companions, ~70% among 15–24 year-olds (Film Companion / TOBAR, 2016).
  https://www.filmcompanion.in/features/bollywood-features/fc-insight-young-cine-goers-rather-watch-movies-with-friends-than-family

## Paying each other is now instant (UPI)
- **21.63 billion** UPI transactions in December 2025 — record month, NPCI data.
  https://entrackr.com/news/upi-records-highest-ever-monthly-transactions-at-2163-bn-in-december-10963201

## Money between friends is awkward
- **21%** of UK adults have paid more than their share because paying separately felt socially awkward (2,000 adults).
  https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/we-lose-an-average-of-2-6-billion-per-year-lending-money-to-friends-and-colleagues/
- Only **38%** of US adults are comfortable discussing bank balances with family and close friends — Bankrate 2024 Financial Taboos Survey (2,502 adults, YouGov). (Not used on the page; backup.)
  https://www.bankrate.com/banking/financial-taboos-survey-2024/

## BookMyShow's history with group payments
- **Split Tickets** (Android update, c. 2016): cost split across the group, each pays via BookMyShow MyWallet.
  https://www.fonearena.com/blog/206341/bookmyshow-update-adds-split-tickets-mini-maps-new-language-support-and-more.html
  https://www.gogi.in/bookmyshow-now-allows-users-to-share-tickets.html
- **Plan It** (June 2017): in-app group planning/chat; company said it came from user requests.
  https://yourstory.com/2017/06/bookmyshow-plan-it/
- **MyWallet shut down** (announced Jan 2018, effective end of Feb 2018) after stricter RBI prepaid-wallet (PPI) rules.
  https://entrackr.com/2018/01/bookmyshow-shuts-wallet/
  https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2018/01/bookmyshow-drops-wallet/
- I found no source showing a UPI-based replacement for Split Tickets. **Check the current BookMyShow app before publishing** — if a split feature exists today, reframe the case study as a redesign of it.

## Competitive scan
- PhonePe Split Expense — split with contacts/groups; manually "mark as settled" if paid outside the app.
  https://cms.phonepe.com/en/myhelp/money-transfers/spilt-expense/what-split-expense-phonepe
  Design write-up: https://www.phonepe.com/blog/design/phonepes-split-expenses-streamlining-group-transactions-with-ease/
- Paytm Split Bills — equal or custom splits; lists movie nights as a use case.
  https://paytm.com/blog/payments/movie-nights-trips-more-split-bills-using-paytm/
- Airbnb split payments (Nov 2017) — organiser pays own share, others get 72 hours, everyone needs the app + account, max 16 people; later withdrawn.
  https://techcrunch.com/2017/11/28/airbnb-launches-payment-splitting-for-group-trips/
  https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-makes-group-travel-easy-with-global-launch-of-split-payments/
